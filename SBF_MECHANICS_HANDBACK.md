# Strategic BattleForce mechanics handback

Working checkout: `C:\Projects\megamek\megamek_temp`, branch `UI_terraincompression`. Baseline inspected: `fa210f38b7`. Fetched the coordinator branch locally. All six supplied commits are ancestors of this checkout. The native 3D board and SBF client phase displays are owned by the coordinator's UI worker.

Rules authority: *Interstellar Operations: BattleForce*, third printing, BattleTech-35006VB. All page numbers below are printed page numbers. The standard ground game is pp. 160–174. Aerospace, advanced options and campaign extensions have separate entries. BattleForce standard (pp. 24ff.), ACAR, Abstract Combat System and Total Warfare are different games. A conversion, stat block, optional-rule flag or UI label alone does not implement a rule in a networked SBF game.

## Initial audit (before this work)

Paths name Java classes under `megamek/src/megamek`; server classes are in `server/sbf`, common SBF classes in `common/strategicBattleSystems` unless specified. IMPLEMENTED means an identified live code path. PARTIAL includes incorrect behavior; the specific discrepancy is recorded. MISSING means no corresponding live SBF resolution was identified.

| Mechanic | Initial status | Third printing and live code evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Unit/Formation stats, conversion, scenario setup | PARTIAL | “Setup”, pp. 162–163; “Force Commander and Formation Leaders”, p. 163. SBFUnitConverter, BaseFormationConverter and SBFFormationDeserializer build stats, assign LEAD for multi-Unit Formations and reject duplicate COM within a Formation. Force-wide single COM selection, map choice and deployment roll are distinct setup steps. ScenarioV2 already reads the correct options type. |
| Preplaced and scheduled deployment | PARTIAL | “Deployment”, p. 163. Formations in a scenario with coordinates are deployed. SBFInitiativeHelper filters out undeployed Formations even for deployment; the SBFGameManager packet switch has no live placement path. The deployment-zone choice, alternating placement, and optional first-turn board entry are missing. |
| Standard turn sequence, initiative, teams | PARTIAL | “Playing the Game” and “Initiative Phase”, pp. 163–164. SBFGameManager rolls through TurnOrdered (2D6 and tie rerolls). SBFPhaseEndManager contains fall-through and duplicate recursive transitions. Movement and firing sort player initiative even when team initiative decides sides. Multiple report-phase placeholders are from other game systems. |
| Unequal numbers | PARTIAL | “Unequal Number of Formations”, p. 164. SBFInitiativeHelper allocates proportional movement, but counts individual owners instead of sides. Double-blind weighting of undetected formations is missing. Combat uses complete sides, not this movement alternation. |
| Basic ground movement, facing | IMPLEMENTED | “Movement Phase” and “Facing”, p. 164. SBFMovePath, SurfaceSBFMoveStep and SBFMovementProcessor support adjacent movement, remaining stationary and server reconstruction; ground facing does not restrict actions. |
| Minimum movement and stacking | PARTIAL | “Minimum Movement” and “Stacking”, pp. 164–165. A mobile Formation can spend its allowance for one legal adjacent hex. Existing path and envelope caps reject expensive first hexes. Endpoint logic miscounts occupants; intermediate friendly stacks must be traversable. |
| Ground costs, terrain, prohibited terrain, roads and levels | PARTIAL | “Terrain” and “Movement Cost Table”, pp. 164–166. SurfaceSBFMoveStep implements some woods, rough, rubble and levels. Urban/infantry, road bonuses, mode-specific levels and water exceptions are incomplete; naval dry land is misclassified. |
| Jump, hover, VTOL, WiGE and naval/submerged movement | PARTIAL | “Movement Modes and Unit Types” and “Additional Movement Rules”, pp. 166–167. JUMP declaration, server validation and attack modifiers exist. Airborne movement vs ground terrain, WiGE upkeep/landing, minimum water depths and submergence have gaps. |
| Engagement, evasion/overrun and ending engagements | PARTIAL | “Engagement Control”, pp. 167–168; “Ending Engagements”, p. 172. Hostile-hex exit costs exist. SBFMovePath unconditionally stops some paths; mutual intent, opposed Tactics, engagement outcomes and persistent engagements need implementation. |
| Transport | MISSING | “Transporting Infantry”, p. 167; “Transporting Non-Infantry Forces”, p. 199. Unit/Formation conversion carries transport ratings. No live embark/disembark, passenger ownership/state, capacity, turn consumption and destruction consequences. |
| Attack declaration, count, server authority | IMPLEMENTED | “Attack Declaration”, p. 169. SBFAttackProcessor validates the acting owner, Formation/Unit, once per Unit and no more than two targets. A player may pass. Standard physical and anti-'Mech attacks are expressly outside p. 169. |
| Attack eligibility, LOS, engagement, range and targeting | PARTIAL | “Types of Attacks”/“Resolving Attacks”, pp. 169–171. SBFToHitData and SBFStandardUnitAttackHandler use skill vs 2D6, target movement/JUMP and ordinary long/extreme ranges. Secondary-target, withheld fire, targeting crit, woods/urban and engagement modifiers are incomplete. Server validates standard attacks only. |
| Damage allocation, criticals and timing | PARTIAL | “Tactics Check”, pp. 170–171; “Critical Hits”, p. 171; “Damage”, p. 172. Handler uses the first Unit and a fixed weapon crit, mutates immediately and lacks removal at End. Defender/attacker choice, two-hit concentration limit, optional random allocation, actual 2D6 table, simultaneous effects and formation movement recalculation need work. |
| Indirect fire, spotting and underwater combat | MISSING | “Types of Attacks”, p. 169; “Underwater Combat”, p. 171. IF/TOR ratings can be displayed; SBFAttackProcessor and standard handler have no corresponding attack/spotter path. |
| Crippling, forced withdrawal, voluntary retreat | MISSING | “Forced Withdrawal”, p. 172. Category-specific thresholds, legal route toward home edge, edge exit and inability to move have no live resolver. Do not equate loss of one Unit or a weapon crit with a crippled Formation. |
| Morale, exemptions, level effects and recovery | PARTIAL | “Morale” and “Morale Recovery”, pp. 172–174. BaseFormationConverter supplies skill + 3, SBFFormation stores status, SBFToHitData reads the defensive modifiers. No End Phase checks, recovery, forced movement, loss of command or rout consequences. ACAR has a different morale sequence. |
| Ground victory, objectives, scoring | MISSING | “Victory Conditions”, “Capturing Objectives” and “Victory Points”, p. 174. ScenarioV2 parses scripted events, but SBFPhaseEndManager unconditionally starts another round. Elimination counts off-map retreats; capture requires two consecutive End Phases. Suggested VP and campaign objectives need independent scenario selection. |
| Salvage | MISSING | “Salvage”, p. 174. Optional final-End-Phase campaign roll with eligibility exclusions; no live SBF salvage resolver. |
| Strategic aerospace setup, movement, combat and End | MISSING | “Strategic Aerospace Rules”, pp. 175–179. Conversion can create aerospace stats; no atmospheric radar map turns, thrust/zone paths, air-to-air engagements, air/ground attacks and aerospace End damage. |
| Advanced aerospace | MISSING | “Advanced Strategic Aerospace”, pp. 180–193. Ground-map aerodynes/spheroids, transports, capital map movement, fuel/gravity, arcs, engagements, capital damage, missiles, boarding, high speed, docking and jumps each require game actions/resolution. |
| Advanced initiative and sensors | PARTIAL | “Advanced Initiative” and “Detection and Reconnaissance Phase”, pp. 194–198. SBFDetectionHelper and SBFDetectionModifiers implement parts of sensor detection and SBFVisibilityHelper publishes per-player visibility. Recon choice, visual detection, loss of contact, hidden activation and initiative effects are incomplete. |
| Advanced formation movement and terrain | PARTIAL | “Advanced Movement Options” and “Advanced Terrain”, pp. 198–203. Sprint already implemented (p. 199). Adjusting Formations, evade, hull down, expanded terrain, bogging, bridges, buildings, rails, ice, snow and extreme water need table-specific verification and live effects. |
| Advanced fire and terrain actions | MISSING | “Advanced Combat Options”, pp. 203–218. Artillery, counterbattery/flak, alternate munitions, battlefield intelligence, boarding, C3, drops, environment, exceptionally large Elements, hidden formations, expanded ranges, mines, urban combat and VTOL attacks. Artillery action classes alone do not resolve fire. |
| Other options, command and special abilities | PARTIAL | “Additional Advanced Options”, pp. 218–226; “Special Command Abilities”, pp. 223–226; “Piloting Special Abilities and Design Quirks”, pp. 226–229; ground/aerospace conversion tables pp. 262–264. SBF conversion and SBFRecordSheet retain many ratings. Terrain/infantry options, drones/remote control, specialist chassis, command/pilot options and ability-specific effects must be traced to live rule consumers individually. |
